Welcome to UNI-COMPASS, an instrument to track collective bargaining in your sector. UNI Europa will aggregate inputs annually to describe evolutions across Europe for its affiliated unions, in order to have a structured base for discussion.

Complete the tables for each of the three main sections: annual results, major agreements and major companies. Your input will be automatically saved. Clicking the 'save' button will also refresh the page for dynamic content.

All fields have comment boxes, allowing you to provide additional information. For instance, you can mention methodological limitations (e.g. how are growth rates calculated), or indicate the scope of the data (e.g. does a pay increase apply to all workers or not), should this be relevant.

For assistance with the completion of your data or the use of this platform, you can reach out to the support staff at HIVA by booking a 20 minute meeting here.
